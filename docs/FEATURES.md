## Movement

| Feature | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walk, sprint, acceleration, friction | working | Numbers in movement_tuning.gd |
| Jump, coyote time, jump buffering | working | |
| Air control, momentum preservation | working | Air steering never slows you down |
| Bunny-hopping and air-strafing | working | A jump timed on landing keeps all speed; strafe+turn in the air gains speed |
| No fall damage | working | By design |
| Crouch | working | Ctrl or C. Lower and slower, fits under 1.4 m gaps, smaller hitbox. In the air it tucks your legs up |
| Slide | working | Crouch while moving fast: speed boost (once per 1.2 s), keeps momentum, speeds up downhill, jump out of it |
| Grapple (momentum-based) | working | Right mouse. Yanks you toward the hook when it catches, then reels you in, building speed toward it (up to 16 m/s from the pull alone); sideways speed is kept, so hooking off to one side turns into a fast, tightening swing. Already faster than the pull? It turns you toward the hook instead of adding speed (gravity on a swing still can). Hooking from the floor pops you up. Near the hook the pull stops and you coast in. Swing, pump with WASD, let go to fly on with all that speed, jump to let go with a kick. Reeling into your own hook point is never a wall slam. 45 m range, 0.6 s cooldown, any surface |
| Wall-run, wall-jump | working | Hold forward along a wall in the air. Low gravity for 1.6 s, camera leans. Jump to kick off. Same wall can't be reused until you land |
| Vault, ledge climb | working | Run into anything 0.45 to 1.25 m high and you vault it, keeping speed. Jump at a ledge up to 2.3 m and you climb up |
| Dive and roll | working | Q (or Alt): lunge toward where you look (+4 m/s, low hitbox), from the ground or once per jump, 1 s cooldown. Landing from a dive rolls you forward keeping your speed; hold crouch to roll into a slide |
| Belly slide | working | Keep holding dive as you land and you slide along on your front instead of rolling (+1 m/s on impact, a little less friction than a slide, weak steering, speeds up downhill). Lowest hitbox in the game (0.8 m) and you can shoot the whole way. Let go (or jump) to get up; let go while holding crouch to go straight into a slide. If the slide runs out while you are still holding dive you crawl (2 m/s), except on a slope steeper than about 10 degrees, where you keep sliding down it. Under a gap too low to crouch in you stay down until you crawl out |
| Float | working | Shift in mid-air: your up-and-down speed is cancelled and you glide in a straight line the way you face, at the speed you had over the ground (at least 5 m/s), sinking slowly (up to 2 m/s), for 1 s; no steering, so it is a steady platform to shoot from. Once per jump (refreshed by landing, wall runs and the grapple). Any other move takes over from it (flip, dive, grapple, wall run, wall jump, ledge), so it also works as a way to stop rising or falling before the next move; Shift again drops you out early. **Rocket boots while floating** do not end it: the burst sends the whole float off the way you press (WASD as you face; forward with nothing held), with all the speed you had plus the burst's, so you can change direction mid-float. Not within 0.5 m of the floor, so Shift as you land is still just sprint. A hard shove knocks you out of it; a bullet only nudges you along. Your legs go straight out to the sides and whirl like a helicopter's rotor (four turns a second) while the top half keeps aiming; everyone sees and hears it. **The legs hit:** anyone within 1.3 m of your hips while you float takes 15 damage and is knocked away (11 m/s, with some lift, so into a wall it can hurt more), once per float each; the host decides every hit, target dummies count too. FLOAT on the HUD drains while you float. Bots do not use it yet. Numbers in movement_tuning.gd (Float), game/combat/float_spin.gd and at the top of robot_animator.gd |
| Flips (air flip dodge) | working | Press jump again in mid-air: flip toward your WASD direction with a small burst, once per jump (refreshed by wall runs and the grapple). A jump pressed just above the floor still counts as a bunny-hop |

## Known issues

- Animations have only been judged from rendered still frames so far, not in motion on a
  real PC. Timing and feel will need a playtest pass.
- The muzzle flashes are still simple placeholders.
- The sound effects have only been checked by measurement and automated tests, not
  listened to on a real PC yet: expect to tune levels in `game/audio/sfx_list.gd`.
- The sniper rifle has no scope or zoom: right mouse is the grapple. It can be added
  with a new key if wanted.
- No LAN address detection on some networks: the lobby may show 127.0.0.1. Use `ipconfig`
  to find your address if so.
