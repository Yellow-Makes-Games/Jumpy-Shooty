# Sound effects: where they come from and their licence

Every sound in `game/audio/sfx/` is generated by code in this repository:
`tools/audio/make_sfx.py` builds each one from noise, sine waves, filters and
envelopes with Python and numpy. No recordings, samples, sound libraries or
downloaded files are used, and nothing was made with a third-party tool whose
licence could apply to the output.

So the sounds are original work owned by this project, and they are safe to ship.
To keep that true, any sound added later should either be made by
`make_sfx.py` too, or be CC0 (public domain) with its source and licence listed
in a table below.
